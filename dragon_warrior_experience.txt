   That's right folks, Strife132 returns to the scene of FAQ writing
with a simple EXP chart for an old classic. I was playing through this
game when I recalled this little card I had a long time ago with all the
levels, how much EXP it takes to get to those levels, and what spells you
learn at what level. I knew this was long lost, but I figured that I'd
surely be able to find one at GameFAQs! So, I hopped online and went to
GameFAQs and, surprisingly, there was not one single specialized FAQ
just for the EXP chart. So I decided to make one myself for those like
myself who don't need a whole walkthrough but rather, are just replaying
this old game and find that an EXP chart makes things much easier. So,
without further ado, the EXP chart.
